27.0°N 74.2°E · Rajasthan, India

Spatio-temporal modelling of climate impacts on crop yield

Peer-reviewed paper · International Journal of Information Technology (Springer), October 2025 · First author

Graph neural networkhow neighbouring districts affect each other LSTM with attentionhow conditions build up over the season
Random-forest baggingcombines both into one yield estimate

I designed a hybrid model for agricultural and climate data from Rajasthan. A graph neural network captures the spatial links between districts, an LSTM with attention captures the timing within a season, and a random-forest bagging step combines their outputs.

r = 0.68 correlation with observed yields, higher than the baseline spatio-temporal models compared in the paper. Also evaluated with MSE, MAE, RMSE and R².

Limitation: the data covers one season (2018 to 2019), so the next step is testing across more years.

  • Graph neural networks
  • LSTM + attention
  • Ensembles

27.5°S 153.0°E · Brisbane

Brisbane bus service equity

Independent GIS project · QGIS · 2025

Which suburbs have plenty of residents but little bus service? I joined ABS 2025 population projections to SA1 and SA2 boundaries, rebuilt every route from Translink's GTFS feed, counted 2.88 million scheduled stop departures and aggregated them to suburbs with spatial joins. Totals matched across the stop, SA1 and SA2 layers.

138 of 227 scored suburbs came out under-served. The top of the list mixes fast-growing fringe areas (Chambers Flat–Logan Reserve, Yarrabilba) with dense inner suburbs such as Kangaroo Point.

Next step: the current score mixes density and frequency on different scales, so I'm replacing it with departures per 1,000 residents ranked within density bands, which is easier to compare.

  • QGIS
  • GTFS
  • ABS SA1/SA2
  • Spatial joins

27.5°S 153.0°E · UQ St Lucia

Fake news detection that holds up under attack

Master's capstone · The University of Queensland · 2026

A detector that reads both a post's title and its image, tested against posts deliberately manipulated to fool it. BERT encodes the title, ViT encodes the image and CLIP scores how well they match. I generated 9,697 adversarial posts with Qwen2.5 and Stable Diffusion, then retrained on clean and adversarial posts with a ViT modified to focus on reliable image regions. Data: Fakeddit, cleaned to posts with valid images.

Manipulated posts0.68 → 0.71
Clean posts0.80 → 0.82

The gains were not statistically significant on the small test sets, so a larger evaluation comes next. The clearer finding: detectors tested only on clean posts overstate how accurate they are.

  • PyTorch
  • Hugging Face
  • BERT · ViT · CLIP
  • Generative AI

12.8°N 80.2°E · Chennai, India

FALLERT: fall detection for older people living alone

IoT wearable · Vellore Institute of Technology · Patent filed

Single-sensor fall alarms go off when someone sits down quickly. FALLERT is worn at the waist and combines an accelerometer, gyroscope, barometer and heart-rate sensor to tell a real fall from everyday movement and estimate how hard the impact was. A NodeMCU (ESP8266) with a SIM800 module sends Wi-Fi and SMS alerts to carers.

Patent filed for the working prototype.

  • Sensor fusion
  • ESP8266

Off-planet · The Moon

Finding craters on the Moon

Team project · Vellore Institute of Technology · 2022

We labelled 29 lunar surface images by hand, cleaned them with OpenCV sharpening and noise filters, and fine-tuned a pretrained YOLOv5 detector for a single crater class over 150 epochs.

0.56 mAP on the validation images.

With only 29 images this is a proof of concept. The same workflow is what I now use for counting sorghum heads in drone imagery.

  • OpenCV
