27.0°N 74.2°E · Rajasthan, India

Spatio-temporal modelling of climate impacts on crop yield

Published in International Journal of Information Technology, 2025

Graph neural networkhow neighbouring districts affect each other LSTM with attentionhow weather through the season shapes yield
Random-forest baggingcombines predictions into one yield estimate

I designed a hybrid model for agricultural and climate data from Rajasthan. A graph neural network captures the spatial links between districts, an LSTM with attention models change over the season, and random-forest bagging combines their outputs.

r = 0.68 correlation with observed yields, higher than the baseline spatio-temporal models compared in the paper.

Limitation: the model was trained and tested on a single season (2018 to 2019) from one state, so it may not hold for other years or regions.

  • Python
  • PyTorch
  • scikit-learn

27.5°S 153.0°E · Brisbane, Australia

Brisbane bus service equity analysis

Independent QGIS project

I joined ABS 2025 population projections to SA1 and SA2 boundaries, rebuilt every route from Translink's GTFS feed, processed 2.88 million scheduled stop departures and aggregated them to suburbs with spatial joins.

138 of 227 suburbs are under-served. The most under-served include fast-growing outer suburbs, Chambers Flat–Logan Reserve and Yarrabilba, and dense inner suburbs such as Kangaroo Point.

Future work: add ABS car ownership data to flag suburbs where few households have a car and buses are critical.

  • QGIS
  • GTFS
  • ABS SA1/SA2
  • Spatial joins

27.5°S 153.0°E · UQ, St Lucia

Adversarial fake news detection

Master's capstone project

I built a detector that reads both a post's title and its image. BERT encodes the title, ViT encodes the image and CLIP measures how similar they are. I generated 9,697 adversarial posts with Qwen2.5 and Stable Diffusion, then retrained the model on clean and adversarial posts using the Fakeddit dataset.

Manipulated posts0.68 → 0.71
Clean posts0.80 → 0.82

Result: adversarial training improved robustness, raising F1 on manipulated posts from 0.68 to 0.71.

  • PyTorch
  • Hugging Face
  • BERT
  • ViT
  • CLIP
  • Generative AI

12.8°N 80.2°E · Chennai, India

Elderly fall detection system

IoT wearable project

FALLERT is worn as a waistband. It combines an accelerometer, gyroscope, barometer and heart-rate sensor to detect a fall from everyday movement and estimate the impact. A NodeMCU (ESP8266) with a SIM800 module sends Wi-Fi and SMS alerts to carers.

Patent filed for the working prototype.

Impact: carers are alerted automatically by Wi-Fi and SMS, so an older person living alone can get help without having to call for it.

  • Accelerometer
  • Gyroscope
  • Barometer
  • Heart-rate sensor
